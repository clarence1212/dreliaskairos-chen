About

Dr. Elias Kairos Chen

Exploring how artificial intelligence, agency and superintelligence are reshaping organisations, economies, governance and the human future.

Dr. Elias Kairos Chen is an AI researcher, entrepreneur, strategic adviser and author whose work spans the science of intelligence and its consequences for organisations, economies and society.

His research background is in brain-inspired artificial intelligence, with doctoral and published work on spiking neural networks and affective computing. That scientific grounding shapes how he thinks about where AI is heading, from today's agents to the debates about general intelligence and superintelligence.

He founded Kiros Intelligence Group in 2005 and leads it from Asia-Pacific, working with collaborators across the United States and Europe. Through the firm he contributes to strategic advisory work with boards and enterprises on AI strategy, agentic transformation, governance and measurable value creation. He is also the founder and CEO of SafeGuardAI, which applies research on adolescent development and privacy-preserving AI to children's digital wellbeing, and he invests in and advises AI companies.

He originated the Age of Agency, Agentrification and Universal Basic Intelligence frameworks, writes the newsletter Framing the Future of Intelligence, and is the author of the forthcoming book The Age of Agency.
