Ideas for boards, leaders and audiences

Keynotes, board briefings and workshops on AI, agency and the future of intelligence, grounded in research and built around the decisions audiences face now.

Proposed topics

Speaking themes

Available topics, tailored to sector and audience. These are proposed themes, not a list of past engagements.

  1. 01

    The Age of Agency

    What boards and leaders must decide as AI moves from answering questions to acting on our behalf, and how to draw the delegation map deliberately.

  2. 02

    From AI Adoption to Enterprise Value

    Why readiness matters more than adoption, and how to judge AI by what it does to revenue, costs, capital and risk.

  3. 03

    The Future of Human Intelligence and Work

    Agentrification, the roles that change and disappear, and how organisations keep developing the leaders of the future.

  4. 04

    Agentic Systems and Responsible Governance

    Decision rights, oversight, accountability and assurance when AI systems act inside organisations.

  5. 05

    Superintelligence and Institutional Transformation

    The pathways being debated from AGI to superintelligence, and what governments, institutions and societies should prepare for.

Speaker kit

Dr. Elias Kairos Chen is an AI researcher, entrepreneur, strategic adviser and author. His research background is in brain-inspired artificial intelligence and spiking neural networks. He is the founder and managing partner of Kiros Intelligence Group, the founder and CEO of SafeGuardAI, and the originator of the Age of Agency, Agentrification and Universal Basic Intelligence frameworks. He writes the newsletter Framing the Future of Intelligence, and his forthcoming book is The Age of Agency.
