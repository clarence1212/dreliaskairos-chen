Dr. Elias Kairos Chen is an AI researcher, entrepreneur, strategic adviser and author. His research background is in brain-inspired artificial intelligence and spiking neural networks. He is the founder and managing partner of Kiros Intelligence Group, the founder and CEO of SafeGuardAI, and the originator of the Age of Agency, Agentrification and Universal Basic Intelligence frameworks. He writes the newsletter Framing the Future of Intelligence, and his forthcoming book is The Age of Agency.
