Strategic advisory through Kiros Intelligence Group

Beyond research and writing, Dr. Elias Kairos Chen contributes to strategic advisory work through Kiros Intelligence Group, supporting boards and enterprises in navigating AI transformation, intelligent systems and responsible value creation.

The approach

The advisory work starts from a simple premise: AI should be judged by what it does to an organisation's results, and by how responsibly its decisions are governed, not by how widely it is adopted.

Engagements are delivered by Kiros Intelligence Group, an independent advisory group based in Asia-Pacific with collaborators across the United States and Europe. They apply three connected frameworks, described below.
